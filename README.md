# Thiên Phú Khai Giới — Realmbreaker: Celestial Genesis

A small, single-player, top-down ARPG prototype set across the nine fractured
regions of Huyền Thổ. It runs directly in a modern browser and has no external
dependencies.

## Play

Open `index.html` in a browser, then choose **Nhận Thiên Phú** to begin. Move
with **WASD** or the arrow keys; attack with **Space**, use your skill with
**E**, activate your randomly-awakened talent with **Q**, and dodge with
**Shift**. The buttons below the arena can also be used for combat actions.

Defeat each region's leader to dispel its fog and claim its territory. Clear all
nine chapters to reveal the sealed talent and choose one of two endings; each
ending unlocks a different endgame mode.
